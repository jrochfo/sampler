Sampler

Sampler turns any picture into a cross-stitch pattern you can actually stitch, with a chart, a list of DMC threads, and a preview of the finished piece.

How to

Add a photo, drop or paste one in, or try a sample. Drag the picture to position it inside the shape, and open Edit to change the shape, the number of stitches and colors, and the fabric. Switch to Chart to see the pattern itself, then Save a picture or a full PDF pattern.

Your pictures never leave your computer. Everything happens right here in your browser.

Threads & credits

Colors are matched to DMC six-strand embroidery floss. On screen they're approximations, so check them against real thread if it matters. Color data from the dmc package by Sharla Gelfand. Sample photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are credited as they appear. Icons are hand-drawn with Rough.js.
